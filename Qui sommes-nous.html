Plateforme numérique intelligente conçue pour la gestion de votre école !

As the world is undergoing a rapid digital transformation across all sectors, including education. In Burundi, the educational system faces several challenges: limited access to pedagogical resources, the lack of a centralized tool for the digital management of courses, exams, and results, and communication difficulties between students, teachers, and educational authorities. The Ministry of Education seeks to address these needs by developing a national educational platform accompanied by an application.

Objectives

It is through this occasion we decided to create a national digital platform for learning and school management that will provide official pedagogical resources; Facilitate communication between students, teachers, and administration. Digitize exams, grades, and report cards. Offer continuous online training.
